About the studio

A Warsaw atelier rooted in Brest

Vera Kutavenka is 22 and grew up in Brest, Belarus, in a family where organizing weddings and celebrations was a generations-old tradition. Watching her family and helping with the flowers and decor from a young age, she found her own passion for floristry and the art of creating unforgettable moments.

For the past three years, Vera has been running Zhyvitsa on her own in Warsaw — a new city and country for her — carrying that family tradition forward and translating it into a contemporary floral language. The studio's name, from the Belarusian word for "resin," captures that continuity: resin is a living substance that protects and preserves what matters most, much like she tries to hold, in flowers, the fleeting moments of her clients' lives.
